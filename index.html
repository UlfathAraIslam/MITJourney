🎉 Fun Learning Activities

🎉 Activity Name 📝 How to Play / What to Do
Word Bingo (Bingo)Create bingo cards with new English words. Call out meanings — match the word! Winner gets a prize.
Story ChainStart with a sentence; others continue. Build a fun, silly, or amazing story together!
English Karaoke Party (Karaoke time)Sing along to favorite kids' songs (with subtitles) on YouTube. Practice pronunciation and rhythm.
Robot WalkGive coding-style commands like "Turn left", "Jump", "Walk 3 steps" — act exactly!
Blockly Puzzle Time (Code.org)Solve coding puzzles on Code.org. Set a timer and celebrate each small win!
Storybird Challenge (Storybird)Write a short story or poem using Storybird.com pictures. Share and read aloud!
Theme Drawing Time (Theme Draw)Pick a fun theme (like "Under the Sea") and draw together. Display the artwork!
Comic Strip Time (Comic Fun)Create a 4-6 panel comic about a funny or silly adventure. Color and share!
New Invention Day (Invent Today-Short presentation)Think of a crazy or brilliant invention. Draw it and give a "Shark Tank"-style pitch.
Public Speaking Club (Talk time)2-minute random topic talks ("If I Had a Dragon 🐉"). Talk confidently while others cheer!
Mini Hackathon2 hours to build something (LEGO robot, simple website). Present the creation proudly!
Blockly Puzzle TimeSolve 1-2 Blockly puzzles on code.org together daily as a "race."
Dream University Poster MakingCreate posters about Harvard, MIT, Oxford — dreams, goals, future careers!
